# Naming candidates

The owner chose the names on 2026-10-20, ahead of a longlist. This file records the chosen names, the alternatives considered, and an obvious-collision web search run before the register searches of D01 T01 §4. It is a web screen, not a trademark search and not legal clearance.

## Chosen

### Rizonesoft Office

The suite is **Rizonesoft Office**. Each app is known in full as "Rizonesoft <App>", as "Microsoft Word" is; inside the app the bare word is its label.

## Alternatives considered

Concepts from the 2026-10-19 brainstorm and the 2026-10-20 decision, kept as the fallback list if a chosen name is blocked.

- **Tessera** (mosaic tile; every icon one tile of a shared mosaic): Tessera Write, Grid, Stage, Flow, Plan, Mail. Not chosen: "Tessera" is a crowded name (Tessera Technologies, the semiconductor IP company now Adeia; Tessera Therapeutics), and it adds a second brand beside Rizonesoft.
- **Rizone** (a short form of the house brand): Rizone Write, Grid, Stage, Flow, Plan, Mail. Not chosen: it splits the house brand the owner already holds into two spellings for no gain.
- **Kestrel** (bird motif; icons as feather and wing forms): Kestrel Write, Grid, Show, Draw, Plan, Mail. Not chosen: Kestrel is an established brand of weather meters (Nielsen-Kellerman), and a second brand beside Rizonesoft.
- **Atelier** (six standalone names, as Apple's Pages, Numbers, Keynote): Inkwell, Abacus, Lectern, Blueprint, Milestone, Courier. Not chosen: six names need six clearances, and Abacus and Blueprint are crowded software names.
- **Rizonetech**: considered as the house brand on 2026-10-20. Not chosen: it is the owner's services company name, not the software brand.
- **Tesserac**: considered on 2026-10-20. Not chosen: it is one letter from the Tesseract OCR engine, and search engines autocorrect it to Tesseract.

## Collisions

Searched 2026-10-22 with a general web search, one query per name. Prominent conflicts only; the register search is D01 T01 §4.

### Rizonesoft

- Search `"Rizonesoft"`: no other company, product or domain using the name found. The results returned only unrelated names with a "-soft" ending (Tynesoft, Cronosoft). The owner's own GitHub organisation, `rizonesoft`, hosts this repository.

### "<house brand> Office" precedent

Suites that use the generic word "Office" behind a house brand, so "Rizonesoft Office" follows an established pattern:

- **Polaris Office** (Polaris Office Corp.): US registration 5398985, POLARIS OFFICE, registered 2018-02-13, with "OFFICE" disclaimed (https://trademarks.justia.com/870/49/polaris-87049755.html). The disclaimer is the precedent: the register treats "Office" as generic for document software.
- **WPS Office** (Beijing Kingsoft Office Software): its related US mark WPS 365 is registration 7683277, registered 2025-02-11 (https://gleanmark.com/trademark/uspto-79396690). No US record for "WPS Office" itself appeared in this search.
- **SoftMaker Office** (SoftMaker Software GmbH): a commercial suite sold under that name (https://www.softmaker.com); no register record appeared in this search.
- **ONLYOFFICE** (Ascensio System SIA): sold as one word (https://www.onlyoffice.com); no register record appeared in this search.
- **LibreOffice** (The Document Foundation): https://www.libreoffice.org; no register record appeared in this search.

### App names

- **Write**: "Microsoft Write" was Microsoft's own word processor, shipped from Windows 1.0 and replaced by WordPad in Windows 95, and also the name of a Mac and Atari ST release of Word (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Write). Retired, but a Microsoft product name; with "Writer" (LibreOffice, Zoho) close by, the strongest conflict of the six.
- **Grid**: GRID (grid.is), a spreadsheet product with its own editor, GRID Sheets (https://webcatalog.io/fr/apps/grid). A live spreadsheet product under the same word.
- **Stage**: Calligra Stage, KDE's presentation app, still released (https://apps.kde.org/calligrastage/); Stage by Belkin, an iOS whiteboard and demo app (https://apps.apple.com/ch/app/stage-by-belkin/id584574701).
- **Flow**: Microsoft Flow, Microsoft's workflow product from 2016 until its rename to Power Automate on 2019-11-04 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Power_Automate). A former Microsoft product name.
- **Plan**: Calligra Plan, formerly KPlato, KDE's project planner, last stable release 3.3.0 in January 2021 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calligra_Plan); Microsoft Planner, Microsoft 365's task tool since 2016 (https://www.techtarget.com/enterprise-software/definition/Microsoft-Planner).
- **Mail**: Apple Mail and Windows Mail, both email clients named "Mail" (https://www.alternativeto.net/software/apple-mail/). The word is generic for email clients; no single owner.

Every app word collides with something, as generic words do; that is why the house brand carries the protection (D01 T01 §3). The conflicts for the owner and for the register search in §4 are Write and Flow (former Microsoft product names), and Grid (a live spreadsheet product of the same name).
