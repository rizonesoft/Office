A prerequisite list says which sections come first, not what their items ask for. A rewiring that removed every graph edge to the later apps' inventories still left six sections whose Done when asked for those inventories in words: a chapter proved on Excel's tab, a decision reading Excel's ribbon file. When a dependency is removed, read every item of every open section on the shortened path for what it needs of the thing no longer ahead of it, restate each around what exists, and give the rest to the section that owns it.
