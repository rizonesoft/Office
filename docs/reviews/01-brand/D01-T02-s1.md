## Dispositions after round 3

Both findings verified. The symbol tests had now been patched two rounds running, so they were re-thought rather than patched a third time. A colour-blind "fully covered pixel" measure was tried first and dropped: it flags real antialiased slivers where two strokes meet (Mail `md`, 2.5:1 at one crease pixel), so it cannot tell a faded part from a crease. The fix closes the input instead:

1. P1 opacity: `test_symbols_use_a_closed_vocabulary` replaces the paint test. A symbol may contain only self-closing `rect`, `circle` and `path` elements with an allowlist of geometry and paint attributes, so opacity, transform, style, filter, clip and mask are refused outright, and every fill and stroke must be the symbol colour or `none` (the Mail `sm` flap the one exception, held to 3:1 against the envelope). With that, every symbol part renders fully in the symbol colour, and the rendered test's colour sampling covers every part.
2. P1 glyph geometry: the same test requires absolute path commands only (`M L H V Z`) and every coordinate, rect edge and circle edge within the 64-unit box (plus or minus 32), and `test_every_symbol_stays_in_its_box` renders each symbol alone, finely within its box and coarsely on a canvas 128 times as wide, refusing any part outside the box.
