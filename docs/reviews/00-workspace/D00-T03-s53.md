## Re-think: one store cannot promise exactly once

The completion review (gap 1) and the reconciliation after it are the two sides of one fact: the digest's only record is the notice queue, and a write to it can fail at any moment around the send. Marking after the send repeats a batch when the mark fails; claiming before the send and trusting the claim loses a batch when the process stops before the send completes. With one fallible store, a notice can be guaranteed delivered at least once or at most once, not exactly once. The section's contract promises the batch is "marked reported only after the send succeeds, never by --dry-run or a refused send", and the product principle that outranks any convenience is that no notice the owner needs is lost. So delivery is at least once: a batch is claimed before the send (nothing is sent when the claim cannot be written), marked after a delivered send, and a claim no send confirmed is released at the next digest's start and sent again in full, labelled "sent before, unconfirmed; it may repeat". A repeat can therefore happen only after a write that failed following a delivered send, and the owner sees that it may be one. Rebuilt in the commit after aebdf89; the tests cover a failed mark, an interrupted send and a failed release, each losing nothing.
