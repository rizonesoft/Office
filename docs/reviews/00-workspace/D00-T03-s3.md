## Dispositions after round 2

All six findings were verified and fixed, each with a self-test case (all six also exist in Isotone's original): observation updates read, merge and replace under a sessions lock (the guard's interprocess lock class on `build/claude-sessions.lock`); an owner leaves a session's record only once proven gone, with no size cap (`an early live owner survives many later observations`); a creation time must be an integer (`a text creation time proves nothing and holds`); the supervisor ends a resume when its own session's record turns unreadable or incomplete; a failed SessionStart observation leaves `unobserved-<session>.flag`, which holds every launch until that session observes again (`a failed observation holds every launch`); and a launch that cannot start returns without touching the resume state (`a launch that cannot start counts no resume`). Residual, recorded rather than fixable here: when the observation and its flag both fail to write, the hook can only report to stderr.
