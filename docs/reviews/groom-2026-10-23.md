## Granularity

Every new section has 4 to 15 micro-steps ending in `Commit:` and a runnable checkpoint; review round 1 found decision items in D03 T01 §14 to §16 that named no file and bundled several services, which were split one decision per item, each naming its record. The authoring sections name one TODO file per subject, each with its contents, so the campaign authors the domains without having to decide scope. Per-control sections cannot be written before the Phase 2 captures exist; the authoring contract and D03 T01 §13's ownership check make the campaign write and prove them.
